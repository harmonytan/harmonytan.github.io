# Hello, Again

This site is intentionally spare: centered type, a few quiet rules, and a simple index of the ideas I am tracing.

## Why keep a log

Writing clarifies the actual questions I carry. Every entry is a timestamped checkpoint—useful when I want to inspect past assumptions or recall how a project took shape.

- Capture the edges of prototypes and experiments
- Leave short field notes from books, podcasts, and conversations
- Track decisions so the context stays available for future me

## How to read this space

Everything is written in Markdown and rendered as-is. Posts stay short, with links and references at the bottom where needed. If something is unfinished, it will say so explicitly.

If any post sparks a thought, send a note. I am always interested in the next question.
