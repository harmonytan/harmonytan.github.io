Biography

I am a student at Peking University. Previously, I interned at JD Explore Academy and Meituan LongCat. My current research interests are AI Alignment and AI Simulation of Humans, especially how AI systems can follow human intent, model human preferences, and reproduce human-like reasoning or behavior in controlled settings.
